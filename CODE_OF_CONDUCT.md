# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Argue about the work, not the person making it. Assume the other party is
competent and short of time, because they usually are.

Specifically, this means:

- Criticism addresses code, methods, and claims. Never identity, background, or fluency.
- Disagreement is expected in research. Contempt is not.
- Someone asking a question that seems obvious to you gets an answer, or a link, not a
  remark about the question.
- English is not everyone's first language. Read past the phrasing to the point.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, publishing
private information, sustained disruption of discussion, and unwelcome attention after
being asked to stop.

## Scope

Every space this organisation runs: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any event or channel where someone is representing Qomira Labs.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@qomiralabs.com**. Reports go to the smallest number of people who can
act on them, and we will not share your identity with the person reported without asking
you first.

We acknowledge within 2 business days. Responses range from a private word, through
removing comments and a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the organisation. We will
tell the reporter what was decided.

Maintainers are held to this more strictly than contributors, not less. A maintainer who
breaks it is handled by the other maintainers.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), v2.1.
