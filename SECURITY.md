# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately to **security@qomiralabs.com**. Do not open a public issue, and do not
disclose the finding anywhere else until we have replied.

Include what the issue is, how to reproduce it, the affected repository or endpoint, and
what an attacker could do with it. If you have a proof of concept, attach it.

We acknowledge within **2 business days** and give an assessment with a remediation
timeline within **10 business days**. If a report affects one of our products rather than
this code, we route it internally and tell you we have done so.

## Scope

Anything in a `qomiralabs` repository, and the qomiralabs.com domain.

Product infrastructure is handled by the product teams. Juncta and CelerFi each run their
own disclosure process; a report sent here that belongs to them will be forwarded, but
going to them directly is faster.

## Good faith

We will not pursue legal action against research conducted in good faith under this
policy: no data exfiltration beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue, no service
degradation, no access to another person's account or data.
